### ron, R75: x², xʸ, x⁻¹ and ÷ after a matrix take the matrix (F68), branch `p1/ron-matrixbase`
**Lane:** night-safe (Opus worker: the entry path; Opus audit). **Starts when:** R74 is merged (c41 and c42 are this package's tests). Backlog F68; found by the core list (sandra, 2026-10-21). Cause, not symptom: a key whose template takes the previous atom (`#@`) does not take a matrix environment as that atom, so the template opens with an empty base after it.
**Read:** `spec/keyboard.yaml` (`div`: primary `\frac{#@}{#?}`, shift `#@^{-1}`; `pow`; `sq`; the one-line forms); `app/src/entry/controller.ts` (`FRAC` line 13, the power-key test near line 39, the DMS argument near 44, and where `#@` is filled); `app/test/tmpl.spec.ts` (`matrix22`, `colvec` fills); `app/test/bksp-grid.spec.ts` and `cursor-grid.spec.ts` (the grid's cursor); `tests/core/core.yaml` c40–c42; docs/04 5.3 (matrices).
- **Probe on `73ea5b5` (sandra, desktop, the core replay with a trace of the entry before Enter):** 2×2 matrix from Tmpl, 1 ▶ 2 ▶ 3 ▶ 4 ▶, then Shift ÷: the line is `\begin{pmatrix}1 & 2\\ 3 & 4\end{pmatrix}\placeholder{}^{-1}` and Enter says "unexpected token at 'placeholder'"; xʸ then (−)1 gives `…\end{pmatrix}-1^{\placeholder{}}`.    - e2e cases in `tmpl.spec.ts`: after a 2×2 matrix, a column vector and a `{ }` list (Shift 8), each of x², xʸ 2, x⁻¹ and ÷ 2 makes the whole group the base or numerator (the entry's LaTeX: `\begin{pmatrix}…\end{pmatrix}^{-1}`, `\frac{\begin{pmatrix}…\end{pmatrix}}{2}`), and Enter gives the value (`source: derived`: [[1,2],[3,4]]² = [[7,10],[15,22]], its inverse [[-2,1],[1.5,-0.5]], halved [[0.5,1],[1.5,2]]);
