### ron, R88: presets, the model and the store, branch `p1/ron-presets-model`
**Lane:** day (no UI; safe at night). **Opus** (it rewrites stored settings and the free keys in one step). **Starts when:** R49, R57, R50e and R4b are merged, so every group a preset holds exists. R24d adds the unit pad's cells to the Your keys group when it lands.

**Why** (owner, 2026-10-27): a preset is a named snapshot of the user's own settings, the free keys included: "a preset for maths and one for chemistry: the fixed keys keep the hand's memory, the free keys give full flexibility". decisions.md "Settings in the Mode sheet, and presets".

**The design:** docs/05 3.4 (Presets); [docs/mockups/settings.html](../../mockups/settings.html) section 4. Five groups, each a tick, all ticked when a preset is saved: `mode` (angle, format, digits, entry), `numbers` (decimal mark, the Fmt cycle, the decimals of Fixed), `look` (family, brightness, high contrast, plain keys), `vibration` (on, strength, length), `keys` (U1–U6 on every layer; the unit pad's cells after R24d). Nothing saves by itself.

**Read:** `app/src/state/session.svelte.ts` (`Settings`, `migrateSettings`, `SETTINGS_SCHEMA`, `save`); `app/src/keyboard/userKeys.svelte.ts` (the stamped store, R15b); `app/src/state/storage.ts` (the keys, `DATA_VERSION`, migrations); `app/src/state/backup.ts` (R38a's `BACKUP_KEYS`, if merged); R50e's theme loader (it awaits its loads); `app/src/state/taps.svelte.ts` (the counters); docs/04 6.2.

1. **Tests first (Vitest), failing on phase-1:** capture holds exactly the ticked groups; apply sets them and leaves every other setting alone; apply, then capture, gives the same snapshot; a preset saved before a later setting existed applies without touching it; the free keys switch and the fixed keys never do; a record stamped newer than `DATA_VERSION` is refused and an older one migrates; `modified` is true after a change to a held setting and false after `update`; two applies in a row end in the second (the theme's loads awaited); every apply is counted.
2. **The model** `app/src/state/presets.svelte.ts`: `capture(name, groups)`, `apply(id)`, `update(id)`, `rename(id, name)`, `remove(id)`, `active`, `modified`; names unique and not empty. Stored under the stamped key `presets`; added to `BACKUP_KEYS` (if R38a is not merged yet, say so in the report and R38a takes it). Each apply counts in the counters (the removal test in decisions.md).
3. **For shared files** (exact text in the report): docs/04 6.2's row for `presets`.

**Done when:**
- The tests fail on phase-1 and pass; typecheck and vitest; the start chunk unchanged (the model loads with the Mode sheet's chunk, R35).
- An e2e that applies two presets through the model (`window.pascaline`) and reloads: the settings and the free keys of the last one survive.
