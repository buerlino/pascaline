1. **Tests first (failing on phase-1):** an e2e `app/test/fold.spec.ts`, by touch and by keys:
   - A tap on the handle folds the keypad; a tap on the slice brings it back. A drag down of ≥ 24 px on the handle folds.
   - A swipe down that starts on the entry line (≥ 24 px, mostly vertical) folds, and leaves the line, its caret and its selection as they were. A horizontal drag on the line still selects, and a tap still places the caret.
   - A swipe up on the slice or the line, Android back, or Esc (after its own jobs: a job, a sheet level, a pick) brings the keypad back. A reload has it up.
   - While folded, a tap picks a row (R147). The second tap inserts and brings the keypad back; Copy and Delete on the picked row do not. A hardware letter types and the keypad stays down. ☰ rises over the history.
   - The folded viewport share is ≥ 0.9, and every counter counts.
2. **The handle:** a grabber bar (32 × 4 px, `--muted`) centred on the keypad's top edge. It is a `<button>` with `aria-label` "Show history" (i18n `keys.fold`) and a touch target of at least 48 × 24 px, taken from the keypad's top padding and the gap above it. No key's hit area shrinks, and the keypad-up viewport share is unchanged (status.spec.ts at 412 × 839, measured in the report). Tab reaches it.
3. **The fold:** the keypad folds at once to a 44 px slice (no slide: docs/05 rule 6, `docs/05-ui-plan.md:23`): its top edge, the handle, the label "Keys", `aria-label` "Show keypad". No key reflows, and the state is not stored. Home and CAS only: an app draws no handle.
4. **The swipe:** a pointer that starts on the entry line and moves ≥ 24 px down, with |dy| > 2 |dx| within 400 ms, folds. The recognizer restores MathLive's caret and selection from the pointerdown (the drag must not select); any other pointer is MathLive's. Up on the line or the slice unfolds. The history's own vertical scroll is not a fold.
5. **While folded:** S25's worksheet W, the history filling the screen with today's rows, and R147's taps. The picked row's buttons (items 1–5) sit on the slice's top edge. Insert, Recall and Store ▶ unfold. A sheet rises from the slice's top edge over the history.
6. **Counters and the removal test:** a named-count counter after `taps.svelte.ts` under the IndexedDB key `uses`: `fold` (by the handle, by a swipe), `pick`, and each action while folded. Settings › Counters (R49's level) shows them as one row, "Results mode ›". P16's removal test stands: under 14 folds or 10 actions while folded in 14 days, the mode goes.
