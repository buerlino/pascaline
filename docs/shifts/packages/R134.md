### ron, R134: shared and why-line texts are plain text: no LaTeX commands, class wrappers or lost constants (F162, F171), branch `p1/ron-plaintext`
**Lane:** A, night-safe, no device. **Sonnet** (an existing pattern, pinned by tests; output text only, no value changes). **Starts when:** –.
**Why:** (backlog F162, F171; sandra's check 2026-11-05 on phase-1 87560c29, `entryText` run under node) the text people paste elsewhere still carries our LaTeX:
- `entryText` (`app/src/clipboard/text.ts:139-164`), used by Share session (`sessionText`, :167-183), has no rule for `\class{…}{…}`, so a unit row with a rare pair (`slot.ts:360` draws it in `pas-rare`) is written `5 \classpas-rareMm` (F162). It has no rule for Greek letters either: `2\alpha+\varepsilon` stays `2\alpha+\varepsilon`, `\theta+\Delta x` stays as is, `G\times\const{alpha}{\alpha}` gives `G×\alpha`, `\const{mu0}{\mu_{0}}` gives `\mu_0`. Rule order bites too: `\mathrm` is dropped before `\to` is read, so `\to\mathrm{m}` becomes `\tom` and `\mathrm{\mu}\mathrm{m}` becomes `\mum`.
- The why line (`app/src/clipboard/whyLine.ts:27-36`, `constantsUsed`) names a constant by its chip's LaTeX (`m[2]`): G times α reads `≈ uses G (± …), \alpha (± …)` (F171). Its pattern `\\const\{([^{}]*)\}\{([^{}]*)\}` cannot match a symbol with braces (`k_{B}`, `\mu_{0}`: `entry/macros.ts:50` says symbols look like that), so such a constant is left out of the line altogether, and the line falls back to "≈ rounded" (to verify in step 1 with μ₀, whose σ is 2.0E-16 in `find/units.yaml:161`).
