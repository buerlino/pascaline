### ron, R112: one key-vibration pulse per touch, branch `p1/ron-onepulse`
**Lane:** day (the owner feels it on the Fairphone; the code and its tests need no device). **Sonnet** (an existing pattern, pinned by tests). **Starts when:** A9 is merged (the plugin's vibration usage).
**Why:** (owner, 2026-11-02, A9's hand check on the Fairphone 6) With Key vibration on, a drag on the arrow pad pulses at every cursor step: `app/src/keyboard/Keypad.svelte` `step()` (about line 229) calls `ontouch()`, so one drag gives dozens of pulses (the phone's `dumpsys vibrator_manager` listed 20 of the app's pulses as `cancelled_superseded`, cut off by the next). A flick up or down pulses twice: at finger-down (`down()`, line 194) and when the flick arms (`move()`, line 226). Two quick pulses are the dead key's "no" (docs/05 3.7, `App.svelte` `dead()`), so a flick reads as a refusal.
**Decided (owner, 2026-11-02):** one pulse per touch, at finger-down. Arrow-pad drag steps are silent; an armed flick shows its bubble and gives no pulse; a dead key keeps its double pulse, the only second pulse; a held repeat key (⌫) stays as it is (one pulse at the press, none per repeat).
**Read:** `app/src/keyboard/Keypad.svelte` (`down`, `move`, `step`, `up`; the `ontouch` prop's comment at line 46); `app/src/keyboard/gestures.ts` (the comments at lines 12 and 28, `FlickTracker.move`'s return); `app/src/App.svelte` (`touched`, `dead`); `app/src/input/haptics.ts`; the e2e helper `recordVibrations` (`app/test/gestures.spec.ts`, `cursor.spec.ts`); docs/05 section 3.7 and its line 36.
1. **Tests first, failing on phase-1** (e2e with `recordVibrations`, Key vibration on): (a) a drag on the arrow pad over at least five steps records exactly one pulse, and the cursor still moves those steps; (b) a flick up on a key with a shift function records exactly one pulse and fires the shift function; the same for a flick down and its letter; (c) a plain tap records one pulse; (d) a dead key still records two; (e) a flick that is armed, slid back and lifted (cancelled) records one pulse and fires nothing.
2. **The change:** `step()` no longer calls `ontouch()`; the flick threshold in `move()` no longer calls it (the bubble stays). If `FlickTracker.move`'s boolean then has no reader, drop it with its tests or keep it for the bubble, whichever leaves less code. Rewrite the comments (Keypad.svelte line 46, gestures.ts lines 12 and 28) so none promises a tick for a step or a flick.
3. **docs/05 section 3.7:** one sentence: key vibration is one pulse per touch, at finger-down; a dead key's double pulse is the only second pulse (owner, 2026-11-02, R112).
