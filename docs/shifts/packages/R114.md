### ron, R114: Ctrl+V and every system paste go through our paste, branch `p1/ron-hwpaste`
**Lane:** B, after G157 (the v0.1.0 bar, release-0.1.0.md point 3; Opus: entry). **Starts when:** –.
**Why:** (backlog F157, R100's probe; code read by sandra on `9cf0881f`; family 7)
- In comma mode, `max(`, then Ctrl+V of `1,5`, then Enter gives 5 (right 1,5: the comma is the decimal mark there, R100). Ctrl+V of `1e-3` enters e − 3 with Euler's e (right 0.001). Both are unmarked.
- **Cause:** `app/src/entry/hwkeys.ts:84` hands every Ctrl, Alt and Meta combination to MathLive (its header, lines 15–16: "copy, paste, select all"), and nothing in `app/src` listens for `paste` (`grep -rn 'addEventListener("paste"' app/src`: none). So MathLive reads the clipboard as maths. The Paste key (Shift + Copy) reads it through `parsePastedNumber` (`app/src/clipboard/paste.ts:84`, called by `pasteNow` at `app/src/clipboard/actions.svelte.ts:157`), which knows the comma mode and E notation and refuses anything that is not a number or a list of numbers.
