### gorge, G81: a constant's σ through its basis (correlated constants), branch `p1/gorge-m4-constcorr`
**Lane:** night-safe (Opus). **Starts when:** G80 and G76; before R40 (it shows `uncDigits`) and R61 (a unit result's σ). From libqalculate #1045 (closed 2026-10-20) and the 2026-10-20 check below.
**Why:** `constUnc.ts` (G26) and the unit view's σ (G18, G41) take every constant as an independent input. Many CODATA constants share α and R∞, so the σ comes out wrong in both directions. With today's index values (mpmath, first order):
- `mu_0*epsilon_0*c^2` is exactly 1. We get 1.00000000000119 with σ 2.2E-10: the rounded values don't cancel, and the σ is not 0.
- `sqrt(mu_0/epsilon_0)` is Z₀: we get σ 4.2E-8, CODATA's is 5.9E-8. Too small by √2, so R40 would show a digit as certain that is not.
- `m_p/m_e`: we get σ 8.0E-7, CODATA's is 3.2E-8. 25× too big, so R40 would mute digits that are known.

**Read:** `app/src/engine/constUnc.ts` (all; `sigmaExpr`, `withConstValues`); `app/src/units/evaluate.ts` (the σ near line 236); docs/04 2.8 and 3.6 (chips carry their own values: the `\const{ID}` paragraph); G80's report (the `basis` and `basisLeaves` fields).
1. **Tests first** (vitest, real engine; expected values `source: oracle`, from mpmath with CODATA 2022's inputs): the three cases above: `mu_0*epsilon_0*c^2` with value exactly 1 and σ = 0, the other two with σ within 2 % of the correlated value. All three fail on phase-1.
2. **Chips:** an inserted `\const` chip copies `basis`, and the `basisLeaves` it uses, into the entry's chip table beside `value` and `unc`. A chip stored before this package (no `basis`) keeps today's path.
3. **Home (`constUnc.ts`):** each inexact constant with a `basis` enters as its basis over `pasl_<leaf>` names; the σ is taken over the leaves (the same first-order sum, now over independent inputs), and the value is evaluated through the basis, so identities cancel exactly.
4. **Units:** the same substitution where the unit view propagates a constant's σ; if it propagates none today, say so in the report and change nothing there.
5. **Docs:** docs/04 (the "independent input" sentences in 3.6 and 4.2) says constants enter through their basis.
**Not in this package:** the mark. A result in which every leaf cancels (σ = 0) still shows `≈` (decisions M4-4). Whether it may become `=` goes under "Needs the user", with this case.
**Done when:**
- the tests of step 1 pass; vitest, e2e and the golden gate pass;
- a result from a chip without `basis` gives the same `uncDigits` as on phase-1 (a test).
