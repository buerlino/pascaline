### annabell, A59: Android's splash and system bars take the Dark background, branch `p1/annabell-darkbars`
**Lane:** B, with a device step (any of the two). **Sonnet** (resource values, pinned by a test). **Starts when:** – (R50e is merged).
**Why:** (owner, 2026-11-02, R50e's phone check on the Tab One in the system's light mode, after "clear data", screen-recorded) the web side's first frame is already Dark, as R50e intends, but Android's own splash is white for about 0.55 s before it (frames 0.58 to 1.12 s), and the status bar stays white over the dark app. `android/app/src/main/res/values/styles.xml`: `AppTheme.NoActionBarLaunch` has `Theme.SplashScreen` as parent with no background colour of its own (white in light mode), and `AppTheme.NoActionBar` sets no bar colours. The Android default look is Dark (`app/index.html`'s inline script: `n ? "dark"`), whose `--bg` is `#0b1020` (`app/src/style.css`, `[data-family="pascaline"][data-brightness="dark"]`).
**Decided (owner, 2026-11-02):** the splash background and the status and navigation bars take the default Dark's background, whatever the system mode; a user who picked Light sees dark, then light. Bars that follow the chosen theme at run time are not in this package.
**Read:** `styles.xml`; `android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml` (which theme each activity starts with); `app/index.html` (the inline theme script); `app/src/style.css` (the Dark `--bg`); androidx core-splashscreen's attributes (`windowSplashScreenBackground`, `postSplashScreenTheme`).
1. **Test first, failing on phase-1:** a Vitest in `app/scripts/` reads `styles.xml` and `style.css` and checks that the splash background, `android:statusBarColor` and `android:navigationBarColor` equal the Dark `--bg`, and that the bars' icons are light (`android:windowLightStatusBar` false, `android:windowLightNavigationBar` false where the API level has it). A change of the Dark `--bg` then fails it.
2. **The change:** one colour resource (`values/colors.xml`) for the Dark background; the splash theme sets `windowSplashScreenBackground` to it; the app theme sets both bar colours and light icons. No Java, no plugin.
3. **Device check:** on a device in the system's light mode, `pm clear`, then a cold start under `screenrecord`: no frame brighter than the dark app between the launcher and the app (the brightness trace as on 2026-11-02: `ffmpeg … signalstats` YAVG per frame), and a dark status bar. The trace's lines in the report.
**Done when:** step 1's test fails on phase-1 and passes on the branch; `./build.sh --dev` builds; step 3's trace shows no white frame; nothing changes outside `res/values/`, the test and the report.
