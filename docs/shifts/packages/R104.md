### ron, R104: `± n %` after a number in the unit view, branch `p1/ron-m5-pmpercent`
**Lane:** night-safe (parser and evaluator input; the unit view exists). **Opus** (units and the parser, P17). **Starts when:** R24c and R71 are merged (the unit view and ± on Shift+`,`).
**Why:** (night review 2026-10-30, fh-5 part a, S3 once ± is reachable, confirmed by its verifier; base `bb90a5e`)
- A relative standard uncertainty cannot be typed as such: a calibration certificate that gives 250 kPa with a standard uncertainty (1σ) of 0.4 % of reading needs σ = 1 kPa worked out by hand, because in units mode `input/parse.ts:514-523` accepts only a number literal after `\pm`, else `parse.pmNeedsSigma` (`:520`, `i18n/enParser.ts:90`), and `%` is no unit (`find/units.yaml:81-83`). Hand arithmetic is where slips happen.
- G18 (decisions.md:150): ± is an independent standard uncertainty (σ). `± n %` keeps that one meaning: σ = |value| · n/100 in the value's unit. It is not a way to type a tolerance limit: a resistor's 4.7 kΩ ± 5 % is a limit a = 0.235 kΩ whose standard uncertainty is a/√3 = 0.135677 kΩ (oracle, fh's verification), not 0.235. A second form for limits is fh-5 part b, which changes G18 and stays with the owner (decisions.md "Needs the user"); this package must not present `± n %` as one.
- Display (G18, `units/uncertainty.ts:2`): σ to 2 significant digits, the value rounded to σ's last digit.
**Read:** `app/src/input/parse.ts` (the `\pm` rule in units mode); `app/src/i18n/enParser.ts`; `app/src/units/build.ts`, `app/src/units/evaluate.ts` (`pasupm`), `app/src/units/uncertainty.ts`; `tests/golden/units.yaml` and `tests/golden/parser.yaml` (the ± rows); R24c's and R71's reports; docs/04 4.2 (± in the unit view); decisions.md G18.
1. **Tests first, failing on phase-1:** parser rows (`source: derived`, the LaTeX the keys type, units as R24c's tokens): 250 kPa ± 0.4 % gives σ = 250·0.4/100 kPa in the form the parser already writes for a literal σ; `2 ± 10 %` with no unit gives σ = 0.2; `-2 ± 10 %` gives σ = 0.2 (|value|); `± 5 %` with no value before it is refused with the existing message; `% ` after a σ that is not a literal (`± x %`) is refused. `units.yaml` cases (`derived`): 250 kPa ± 0.4 % has σ = 1 kPa in the value and shows `250.0 ± 1.0 kPa`; `2 ± 10 %` shows `2.00 ± 0.20`.
2. **The rule:** in units mode, `± n %` directly after a number (with or without its unit) gives σ = |value| · n/100 in the value's unit; elsewhere `%` keeps its meaning. Home and CAS unchanged.
3. **The keys:** `%` is already on the Shift palette; if the unit pad's σ cell has room for `%`, say so in the report (no layout change here).
**Done when:**
- The tests fail on phase-1 and pass; typecheck, vitest, e2e, the golden gate (parser, units) and the core list pass.
- docs/04 4.2 gains one line (exact text in the report): "`± n %` is σ = |value|·n/100, a standard uncertainty (G18); a tolerance limit ± a is not σ (its standard uncertainty is a/√3 for a rectangular distribution) and is not typed this way."
