### gorge, G117: a range a..b is not a decimal (F83), branch `p1/gorge-rangeliteral`
**Lane:** night-safe (Opus; `app/src/engine/`, no engine patch). **Starts when:** now. From backlog F83 (G99's report). Family 8 (`docs/research/bug-patterns.md`).
**Why:** CAS `sum(x^2,x=1..3)` reaches Giac as `sum(x^2,x=(3/10^1))` and errors: `toExactLiterals` (`app/src/engine/homeEval.ts` 306, through `rewriteDecimals` and `mapNumbers` in `app/src/engine/giacPrint.ts` 360) reads the `1.` and `.3` of `1..3` as decimals. Home shows ≈ 14 (F73). Giac's `..` is the range of sum, seq, int and every `x=a..b`.
**Read:** `app/src/engine/giacPrint.ts` (`mapNumbers`); `app/src/engine/homeEval.ts` (`rewriteDecimals`, `toExactLiterals`, `restoreLiterals`); `app/src/engine/homeEval.test.ts`; the other callers (grep `mapNumbers` and `toExactLiterals` in `app/src/engine/`); `tests/golden/precision.yaml` and `tests/golden/home.yaml` (the case shape, `kind: "cas"`); bug-patterns.md family 8.
1. **Probe first** (`tools/probes/engine.ts` and the app's path): `sum(x^2,x=1..3)`, `seq(k,k=1..4)`, `int(x,x=0..1.5)`, `sum(1/k,k=1..10)`, `1.5..3`, `x=0..1e1`, in CAS and Home; name in the report each that reaches Giac wrong, and every other caller of `mapNumbers` that sees a range.
2. **Tests first, failing on phase-1:** unit tests: `1..3` stays `1..3`; `1.5..3` rewrites only the 1.5; `0..1e1` rewrites only the 1e1; `restoreLiterals` gives the typed text back for each; golden cases: CAS `sum(x^2,x=1..3)` = 14 exact (source derived) and one with a decimal bound (value from `tests/golden/oracle.py`).
3. **The fix:** `mapNumbers` reads `..` as an operator: a number ends before `..`, and a number may start right after it. No other rule changes.
**Done when:**
- The new tests fail on phase-1 and pass; the golden gate passes; the report gives the probe table before and after and the backlog text that ticks F83 (or what is left for F73).
