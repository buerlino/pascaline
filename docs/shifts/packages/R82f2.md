**What the owner saw** (2026-10-27, after R82f1): on the Fairphone 6 (1116 px at DPR 3 = 372 CSS px, font scale 1.0) the status line reads `Function ▾ Sy… P… N… RAD STD 12 ☰`. The view tabs are the only way to change view (R15c1: no Symb, Plot or Num key), so the owner could not tell Plot was a tab, pressed ☰ in Symb (whose Menu is Settings alone) and found no Root.

**Measured** (dev server, `?engine=fake`, the header's boxes; widths 360, 372, 393, 412; every place; chip states):
- The cause is the flex rules in `app/src/App.svelte` (the `header` styles): `.chips` and `.menubtn` are `flex: none`, `.place` is at least 96 px, so `.tabs` (`flex: 0 1 auto; min-width: 0`) absorbs every missing pixel and ellipsizes. The three tabs need 124 px; the chips take 108–138.
- Function: all three tabs cut at 360 and 372; they fit at 393 and 412 in the default state. e2e and core.spec run at 412 (Pixel 7), so no test ever saw it. With α🔒 the tabs are cut at every width measured, Solve's too.
- While the engine starts, "Starting the engine…" joins the chips (254 px): the header overflows by 17–50 px at 360–393 and ☰ is pushed past the screen's edge. "The engine failed to start." does the same for good.
- Nothing has a fixed slot, so pressing Shift or Alpha moves the tabs under the finger.

**The design** (owner, 2026-10-27, from two rounds of mockups): [docs/mockups/status-line.html](../../mockups/status-line.html) sections 1–3. Left to right:
1. **The place's glyph tile** (30×22 px, `--chip-bg`, the maths font) and ▾, opening the apps list as today: Home a house (inline SVG), CAS `∫`, Function `ƒ`, Solve `x=?`. Home and CAS have no views, so their name stands beside the tile. The glyphs live in one table: each `AppEntry` (`app/src/apps/registry.ts`) gets a required `glyph`, Home and CAS theirs beside it. While busy or starting, the tile wears a 2 px ring (today's `⟳` goes).
2. **The views as icons** in one segmented control: Symb `ƒ=` (maths font), Plot a curve on axes, Num a table (inline SVG, `currentColor`); the selected one filled. Each keeps `role="tab"`, `aria-selected`, `data-testid="tab-<view>"`, and gets the view's name as `aria-label` and `title` through the i18n table.
3. **A fixed ⇧ α slot**, 16 px in every place, empty or showing ⇧ (`--shift`) or α (`--alpha`) once, underlined when locked, `a` for the lower-case lock; `mod-shift` and `mod-alpha` test ids kept.
4. **The right side exactly as today:** the angle chip (a tap switches RAD and DEG), the format and digits pill (taps open Mode), ☰.
5. **The engine's start and failure notes leave the status line** and show in the stage.
