   - A tap on a result picks its row (`data-selected`, the row buttons shown) and leaves the line as it was. A second tap on the same result inserts it as a chip and drops the pick. The same holds for an input (recall).

2. **The taps:** History gets `onpick(index)`. The first tap on an `.in` or `.result` button picks the row and loads the clip code, as ▲ does. A tap on the picked row's own `.result` inserts (`oninsert`); on its `.in` it recalls (`onrecall`). The σ line (R40), the reason line (R124) and a unit result's alternative chips (R62) keep their own taps. The keys do not change (▲ picks, Enter inserts; `historyKey`).

With text on the line (a touch pick can happen there; ▲ cannot), they stay above the line as today. The why line stays above the buttons.
