### gorge, G138: `approx` or `evalf` of a finite value past the range is refused as an overflow, branch `p1/gorge-approxoverflow`
**Lane:** night-safe (Opus: app/src/engine). **Starts when:** the next weekly extreme night (lane B; `exp(10^20)` is an extreme input, brief section 0), with G141 and G142; after A53 (the audit then sees the behaviour diff).
**Why:** (night review 2026-10-30, vg-3, S1, confirmed by its verifier; vvg-1 for the underflow side; base `bb90a5e`)
- Home `approx(exp(10^20))` and `evalf(exp(10^20))` give `{"ok":true,"path":"exact","display":"+infinity","approximate":false}` with facts `exact:false, reliableDigits:null`: an unmarked +infinity. The bare `exp(10^20)` gives "Overflow: the number is too large". CAS `approx(exp(10^20))` gives the text `+infinity`, no numeric part. Rerun: `FACTS=1 node tools/probes/engine.ts 'approx(exp(10^20))'` and `MODE=cas FACTS=1 node tools/probes/engine.ts 'approx(exp(10^20))'`.
- Reach: `exp(10^20)` then Shift+Enter, which inserts `approx(#0)` (`spec/keyboard.yaml:231-234`).
- Cause (reading): `overflowAt` (`engine/novalue.ts:128-131`) returns false when the exact output is already infinite (`!infinite(output)`), and `approx`/`evalf` make Giac's exact output `+infinity` at once; `homeEval.ts:786` is the only overflow refusal; `format.ts:418` gives no mark to a value-less result that is not `approximate`.
- Expected: a refusal with its reason, by decisions.md:71 (F50/F51: "a value through a division by zero, an overflow or a lost answer is refused with its reason, never Giac's text"). exp(10^20) is about 10^(4.34294481903E19) (10^20·log10(e), `derived`), past MPFR's exponent range. Source of the expect: `derived`.
- vvg-1: `approx(exp(-10^20))` shows ⚠ 0 (`warn: unchecked`): not false, but the wrapper takes the result off the path where G126's underflow refusal runs; the bare `exp(-10^20)` was not run (to verify in step 1).
**Read:** `app/src/engine/novalue.ts` (`infinite`, `overflowAt`, `lostFloat`, the underflow rule of G126); `app/src/engine/homeEval.ts` (the exact path, `refuse`, around `:780-792`); the CAS path in `app/src/engine/host.ts` (where `numeric` is built); `app/src/engine/format.ts` (`markOf`); `tests/golden/home.yaml` (the `g78-overflow-*`, `g84-*`, `g123-overflow-*` rows); decisions.md "G78f1, G68" and F50/F51.

A `limit`, a division by zero or a typed `inf` keep today's answers.
