### Units (milestone 5)
- **Angles in the unit view (decided 2026-10-19, the owner; deep check E3, E4):** an angle is a quantity both ways: asin, acos and atan give an angle (shown in the mode's unit); an end slot may drop one rad when no cycle unit (rpm, rev, Hz) is on either side, with a note "rad = 1 dropped"; rpm or rev times a length keeps its value (a feed stays right) with a note naming 2π·r·n. Built by G40: the rpm note goes on rpm or rev times a dimension other than time (`3000 rpm × 0.1 m`, `rpm × N·m`), not on rev/s, rpm/s, rpm × min or Hz (λ·f = c is right); asec, acsc and acot give an angle as asin does (Opus pass 33, the worker's defaults; G2 item 68 changes no code, S12). Why: in DEG `asin(1/2)·1 rad` gave `= 30 rad`, `3000 rpm × 0.1 m → m/s` gave `= 5 m/s` (31.4159265359 m/s is the peripheral speed), and `10 rad/s × 0.5 m → m/s` was refused. Would change if: G2 item 68 shows the G2 refusing both, and you then want every rad mismatch refused.
- **G37 (decided 2026-10-19, the owner, check finding 12): °C and °F inside a compound unit are a temperature interval** (1 °C = 1 K, 1 °F = 5/9 K), and the reading says "per degree Celsius (difference)"; alone they keep their offset. Why: it is the only reading `J/(kg·°C)` or `W/(m²·°C)` has, and it removes a refusal on a common unit. Package G37 (gorge, Opus, about half a day, golden cases). Would change if: you want the K spelling forced, so that a slip like `J/kg·°C` for an absolute temperature cannot happen. A result without an end slot whose slots hold an interval °C or °F is shown in SI (`W/(m²·K)`), not as typed (Opus pass 33 default); S11's result view may keep the typed unit.
- **P15 (2026-10-14; G15's check passed): the unit view computes on Giac,** in the worker already loaded, with the Find index's exact SI factors times base-dimension symbols; a result that is not one number times one product of dimensions is a mismatch; temperatures, logarithmic units and first-order uncertainty are our rules (G17, G18); libqalculate stays at build time only, as the index's source.
