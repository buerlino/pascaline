Pascaline themes

Five theme families, each in light and dark, and two switches that work on every one of them: High contrast and Plain keys. The phone below is live: change the strip above it, or tap the rows on its own Settings page (scene “Settings”). Every colour is checked on this page against the same rules as app/src/style.test.ts. Our own colours plus three MIT-licensed palettes; nothing from any HP skin.

Theme = family + brightness.Pick a family (Pascaline is the default), then System / Light / Dark as R12c planned. Every family has both, so the System setting works with all of them.
High contrast: a switch, not a theme.It deepens the grounds, puts every text at 7:1 and outlines each key, in whichever family you use. On at first start when the phone asks for more contrast.
Plain keys (your “no syntax” mode).One neutral tone for all keys, digits a step brighter. Bold always means Shift, italic always means Alpha. ⚠ and errors keep their colour, so a warning never blends in.

What changed from R50a, and why

    
    
      
  • Pascaline is new and takes its colours from the logo: Shift is the amber of the P, Enter the logo blue. Graphite is R50a’s calm grey, refined. Slate and R50a’s High contrast palette are gone: Nord covers the cool blue-grey, and High contrast is now a switch on every family.
    • 
      
  • The real keypad is drawn: the R3 cluster (U1–U6, the round pad, View · Esc · Undo · Copy · ⇤ · ⇥) and rows 3–9 as in spec/keyboard.yaml, with Shift legends top left and Alpha letters bottom left (P7).
    • 
      
  • A taller entry line (52 px) for feedback #54.
    • 
      
  • Borrowed palettes are adjusted only where they fail, and only in lightness (hue kept): Solarized moves 7 colours (its accents are low-contrast by design), Nord 5, Gruvbox 3. The list is in each family’s card.
    • 
      
  • Plot curves keep their colours in Plain keys; they need them to be told apart.
