Three palettes, light and dark

2026-10-18, R50a (feedback #53). Our own colours; nothing here comes from any HP skin or screenshot. Each palette is drawn on one scene at phone size (412 px wide) in light and dark: the status line with chips, three history rows (an exact result, a ≈ result and a ⚠ row; the first row is the picked one), the entry line with an Ans chip, the R3 keypad (cluster and three maths rows, Shift legends top right, Alpha legends top left), a key that is on (Shift), a dead key (sin) and a pick sheet over the maths rows. The contrast numbers and the token tables are computed on this page from the same values that drew the scene. Nothing is built; nothing under app/ changed.

Your pick (backlog “Needs the user” item 7): look at the three on the phone and say which one, or which mix (for example Graphite’s keys with Slate’s accent). R50b then writes the picked values into app/src/style.css.

Jump:

How the numbers are made

  • Contrast is the WCAG ratio on sRGB, the same formula as app/src/style.test.ts: text, Shift and Alpha legends at least 4.5:1 on every key kind; a dead key’s face at least 3:1 and at least 1 less than a live face; the sheet’s labels, numbers and reasons at least 4.5:1 on --panel and --key-num. The new roles are held to the same bar: the ≈ and ⚠ marks, the error row and the picked row’s text at 4.5:1, the focus ring and the high-contrast key outline at 3:1 (non-text). The high-contrast palette uses 7:1 for text.
  • Colour vision: the Shift and Alpha legend colours are run through the Machado et al. (2009) simulation for protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia (full severity), then compared by CIE76 distance ΔE. The bar here is ΔE ≥ 25 (clearly different at legend size); Shift and Alpha also sit on opposite top corners of every key, so colour is never the only difference. Shift is always a warm amber/orange, Alpha a cool blue, the pair that survives red-green deficiency best.
  • The six roles marked new are not in style.css today: --approx, --warn, --pick, --focus, --key-on, --on-key-on. Today’s app draws these with --shift, --error or opacity; R50b adds the ones the owner wants.
