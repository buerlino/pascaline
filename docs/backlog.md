### Is it on track?
**Yes on scope and speed, but with two growing debts.**

- **Speed.** The plan sized the MVP at about 6–9 person-months. All three were done after two days of agent shifts.
- **Performance.** Every bar is met on the low-end Tab One with room to spare (milestone 3 numbers, docs/shifts/2026-10-11-m3-report.md): keyboard usable 662 ms (bar 1 s), engine ready 995 ms (bar 1.5 s), Plot 56 fps (bar 30), solve p95 80 ms (bar 300 ms); Home Enter → result p95 92 ms (bar 150 ms, milestone 1).

20. **F10. ⌫ stuck at a bracket** (bug, owner 2026-10-14, feedback #39). `log(log(□))`, then ⌫ ⌫ ⌫ stops at `log )`, and every further ⌫ does nothing. **Cause:** MathLive's `onDelete` for a `\left…\right` pair, with the cursor at the start of its body: while the closing bracket is real, it sets the opening one to `.` (invisible) and leaves the cursor where it was. The next ⌫ takes the same branch again, forever. Shift + ⌫ (Del) at the end of a body is the mirror case. It hits every bracket on our line, not only log (Playwright, 2026-10-14): `log(2` ⌫ ⌫ sticks at `\log\left.\right)`; `( )` the same; ⌫ right after the opening bracket of `sin(23)` or `5(2)` sticks at once.
