Unit pad

Package S5, 2026-10-17. The measured arrangement of spec/unit-pad.yaml (which units get a cell comes from how often engineers use them), drawn over the keypad, with the six user cells on U1–U6. Tap the cells: it works. Flick a cell up or down with the mouse or a finger, or use Shift or Alpha first, like the G2's taps. Nothing here computes a result.

How to read it

  • Big letter = tap. Top left (amber) = flick up, or Shift then the cell. Bottom left (blue) = flick down, or Alpha then the cell.
  • Teal cells are prefixes, on the number keys from the biggest (G) to the smallest (p). A prefix lights up and waits; the next unit takes it. The same prefix again drops it.
  • Units side by side multiply (N then m = N·m). ÷ puts the rest under the line, once per slot. ² ³ and ⁻¹ act on the unit before; √ on the unit after.
  • Enter goes to the next slot; flick up on Enter evaluates. ◀ ▶ also move between slots. ⌫ removes the last piece.
  • Math opens Find on units, for everything that has no cell (Å, mmHg, cfm …).
  • The line under the formula reads the unit in words with its dimension. It is the only guard against a slip to the neighbouring prefix (M for m).

What the measurement proposes

  1. One pad for every engineer (your call, and P14). The same unit is always on the same cell, so the hand learns it.
  2. 20 unit cells, chosen by use. Counted in 120 engineering articles, 40 each from mechanical/civil, electrical and process/HVAC, weighted equally. The same 20 quantities come out however the counts are weighted.
  3. New on the pad: Btu, TR, bbl, knot, mph, mi, yd, mil, atm, ksi, Torr. Gone: C, Ah, Wb, kgf (Find), H and eV (now user-cell defaults).
  4. The user cells are the user keys U1–U6 while a slot is open: a tap and two flicks each, 18 slots, no shared cell taken. Defaults: the most used units without a cell (dB, H, ton, cal, eV, d, P, St); 9 slots stay free for your own units.

For you to decide

  • K or °C on the tap. Recommended: K (the SI unit, and the one inside W/(m·K)); °C is one flick up. °C is used 1.65× as much as K, under the "twice as much" that would give it the tap. With taps only, °C on the tap would save 0.025 presses per unit.
  • Your own units. The process/HVAC list here is assumed from the counts. Tell me the units you type that take more than 2 presses.
