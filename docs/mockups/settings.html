Mode sheet and Settings: one sheet

The owner's pick of 2026-10-27 after one mockup round (option 4, with option 8 only where it saves a press and option 10 as presets). Settings becomes the next level of the same pick sheet as Mode and every ☰ menu, over the keypad's maths rows, so the top cluster stays live. The right side of the status line stays as today: a tap on RAD switches the angle unit, the format pill opens Mode. No long-press shortcuts. Built by R85 (Shift + Fmt), R49 (the levels), R86 (Mode values in sight), R57, R50e, R87 and the rows of R4b, R24d, R38a, R38b, R59, R70, R53, R37 and R15c4; presets by R88 and R89.

1 · The Mode sheet (R86; R85 adds Shift + Fmt)

Opened by the format pill (on Format), the digits chip (straight on the digits list) or Shift + Fmt (on Angle). Angle, Format and Entry show every value: a tap on a value sets it and closes the sheet; ◀ ▶ move the mark and keep the sheet open; Enter closes. Digits keep their list of presets. Settings › is the last row. 3 → 2 marks a change that saves a press; Settings' own rows stay ◀ ▶ rows, ticks and switches, where buttons would save nothing.

2 · Settings: the next level of the same sheet (R49)

Mode's last row and every ☰ menu's last row open Settings as a level of the sheet; the title shows the path. Digits pick a section, ▶ or Enter opens it, ◀ or Esc goes back one level onto the same row, a tap above the sheet or × closes it, Android back goes back one level. The top cluster stays live: the pad moves, Esc goes back, U1–U6 stay in view. Seven sections, each at most seven rows of 44 px: 7 × 44 + 32 + 8 = 348 of the 380 px at 372 × 767. A row whose package has not landed is left out.

4 · Presets (R88, R89)

A preset is a named snapshot of the user's own settings. It holds any of five groups, each a tick, all ticked when it is saved: Mode (angle, format, digits, entry), Numbers (decimal mark, the Fmt cycle, decimals), Appearance, Key vibration, and Your keys (U1–U6 on every layer and the unit pad cells). The fixed keys never change; the free keys switch with the preset, so the hand keeps every fixed key and the user gets a calculator per job (owner, 2026-10-27). Nothing saves by itself: after a change the preset's name carries a dot until Update or another preset.
