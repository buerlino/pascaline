The owner's decisions of 6 November 2026, drawn on the real layout before anything is built. Use the switches to see each state. Every phone keeps its own palette, whatever this page's theme.

Menu mark

The menu mark: ▾ (picked 6 November)

A key that opens a menu instead of typing gets a small mark after its legend: Vars, Math, Tmpl, Mem, ≤≠, α∞, Mode and Settings. The place name on the status line already uses ▾ for "opens a list", and on a maths keypad "…" can read as "and so on" (1, 2, …). R143 builds ▾; the switch above still shows the other option for comparison.

What changed, and where it is built

  • R140 N62 is black or white with your teal: Enter is teal with a black label, Shift is a bright orange, Alpha is the teal. R150 Teal (new; with N62 and Solarized the only three families: Graphite, Gruvbox and Nord go) is N62 inside out: the whole screen is your teal and N62's black is its text; Dark has N62 Dark's black keys, Light N62 Light's white keys. On the teal only near-black text reads (black 6.4:1, white 3.3:1), so ≈, ⚠ and errors are near-black there and their glyphs carry them. Shift, Alpha, Fmt, ⌫ and the pad now share the other keys' colour; only their label carries the layer colour.
  • R141 The pad is a true circle, without the dashed ring. High contrast no longer outlines the four arrow zones.
  • R142 Letters that type a variable are italic in every mode, and there is no bold anywhere. Plain keys only turns the colour off.
  • R143 The menu mark, your pick above.

The top cluster and the history

  • R146 The keys are Help | Esc, Undo | Redo, Copy | Paste, with Shift giving Settings | Clear, ⇤ | ⇥, ⇤□ | □⇥. Help sits in the G2's own cell, dimmed until it is built.
  • R53 There is no View key. The bar above the keys folds the keypad down with a tap or a drag, and so does a swipe down on the entry line.
  • R147 The first tap on a result picks it, and its buttons take the entry line's place. A second tap inserts it.
  • R148 Gaps between keys are 4 px instead of 6, so the history gets more room.
  • R144 (not drawn) A tap on any key outside an open menu closes the menu.
  • R149 (not drawn) Alpha + ( ) gives N, then 6, 2 and Enter roll the credits.
