Visual pass: mockups for the owner

R12a. Static drawings at 360 px wide (a phone is about 412), dark and light. Nothing here is built; open this page on the phone. The keypad is layout D (R15b): every key at its place, nothing moved. Colours are today's tokens, so any change you ask for is a token change in R12b.

What I propose to change (R12b, after your notes)
  • History rows (3.5): a divider and more space between entries; input muted and left, result right; a long line fades at its edge with “…”; the picked row gets a tinted card, an accent bar on its left and the context bar (Copy, Paste, Delete, Edit) under it; an error row names the problem in one line and underlines the failing part.
  • Sheets: a highlight bar with an accent edge, digits in the shift colour, disabled rows dimmed with the reason below, a count when the list scrolls.
  • Settings: sections with small headings, switches and ◀ ▶ values of at least 44 px; Theme is a three-way control (Dark / Light / System), shown highlighted in screen 6.
  • Plot: function legend in a small card at the top left, labelled points, the tools bar at the bottom.
  • Tokens: one file, a role per colour, the type scale 28 / 26 / 22 / 15 / 11, 4 px spacing. The mockups use the dark and light values of today's style.css unchanged.

Questions for you: (1) is the picked-row card too heavy, or right? (2) should the results be bigger and the inputs smaller than here? (3) a stronger colour difference between nav (green) and control (brown) keys, or keep it quiet?
