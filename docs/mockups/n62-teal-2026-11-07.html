N62, in teal

The teal family takes the name N62. Today's black-and-white family stays, named Black & White. Everything is flat colour now: no gradients, no glow, no depth. Use the switches to arm Shift or Alpha, empty the history, and try the keys with and without a shadow line.

The outline

One solid colour that says what the keypad will do

At rest the entry field has a 2.5 px outline in the logo teal (1 px today). Arm Shift and it turns orange, arm Alpha and it turns bright teal, and on an error it turns red; with news it grows to 3.5 px. While the engine works, one short white piece of the outline runs round it.

All of it is flat colour, with no glow and no blend. With reduced motion set, the running piece stands still.

The keys

Flat, with an optional black line under each key

The keys are flat fields of colour. The black line is a solid 2 px line under each key, with no blur. In Dark it is pure black; in Light it is black at 30 %, so it reads as a shadow and not as a frame.

Without the line the Light keys nearly melt into the screen (1.1:1 key to screen), which suits the uniform look but makes the keys harder to aim at. Try both with the Key edge switch.

One colour family

Every colour is the logo teal at another lightness

The logo teal #529997 has a hue of 193° in OKLCH. Every colour in both N62 themes uses that same hue, from the darkest screen to the brightest Alpha. Only Shift's orange and the error red stand outside it. #529997 itself is Enter in both themes, the Home tile, and the outline in Dark.

In Light the outline is the same teal one step deeper (#368281), because #529997 on the pale screen measures only 2.5:1, below the 3:1 the tests ask for lines. Light's Alpha moved one step deeper too (#005b5a), so that an armed Alpha still differs from the outline at rest.

The mark

The app icon on the Home key, and faintly on an empty screen

The Home key shows the logo as on the app icon: the black mark on a small logo-teal tile. A black mark alone would vanish on Dark's screen; on the tile it reads in both themes. On an empty history the mark sits in the middle, a shade darker than the screen and only just visible. A tap on it shows "N for number, 6 first perfect number, 2 first prime number".

Decided (owner, 7 November 2026)

  1. Families: N62 is this teal family. Today's black-and-white family stays as Black & White, Solarized stays, and R150's Teal goes.

  3. Key shadows become a switch in Appearance, on by default.
  4. Number keys are a step lighter than the other keys.
  5. The logo: the black mark on the teal tile on Home, and very faint on an empty history.
