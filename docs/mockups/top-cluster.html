Top cluster layouts

R15a. Static mockups for the owner's pick (P12 points 2 and 3, open point B). Each layout is drawn dark and light at 206 px wide, half scale of a 412 x 915 px phone. Tinted outline around "Home ⇄ CAS" = the enlarged hit area. Nothing here is built.

Common to all three layouts

  • No Home key and no CAS key. The status line's place name is a button: one tap switches Home and CAS.
  • Hit area: the drawn button stays inside the 28 px line, the tap target is 96 x 48 px (the line plus 20 px below it, 8 px each side of the name). The 20 px below is invisible and takes taps from the top of the history on the left 96 px only, so a tap on a formula in that corner goes to the button, not the formula. R15b must check this against the tap-into-formula flow.
  • The Mode sheet is a chip on the line (as built). Mode is also on Shift + Apps (a keys-only path, S2); Settings stays the last row of Mode.
  • The Apps sheet lists 1 Home, 2 CAS, then the apps. That gives Home and CAS without the line and without a new flick.
  • The maths rows 3 to 9 do not move. Esc, Cancel (with its Shift layer Undo), View (Shift = Copy) and Menu (Shift = Paste) keep their ids and layers.

The fallback if the line is slow

Open point B: if after a week the owner finds the 28 px line far from the thumb or slow, the switch moves to the keypad without a new key: a flick on Apps (down = Home or CAS, whichever you are not in; the Shift layer is already Mode). The Apps sheet path (Apps, 1 or 2) works in every layout from day one and needs no new gesture. The line button stays; it just stops being the only fast way.

Recommendation

B. It is the only layout that keeps every G2 key press the same (Symb, Plot, Num stay one key each) and still gives back a row (about 6 points of viewport share, 0.354 to about 0.415). A gives nothing back. C costs presses to save no more than B. The price of B is the pad: one row of four arrows instead of a cross; the thumb habit is a flick or a drag over the whole row, and Help loses its cell until milestone 6. If the owner will not give up the cross, A is the safe answer and the rows wait.

  • Cross or flat row of arrows (A or B)?
  • If B or C: Esc and Cancel move to the row ends (B) or next to Apps (C). Keeping them as neighbours of the arrows (B) is my choice.
  • Hit area of 96 x 48 px, with the 20 px below the line taking taps from the top-left of the history: accepted?
