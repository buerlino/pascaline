Top cluster layout

R15a, revised 2026-10-14. The owner turned down layouts A, B and C (they are in git history): the arrows stay a pad, stacked as today; no empty cells; a clear order; and free keys that we or the user can set later. Layout D does that. Drawn dark and light at 206 px wide, half scale of a 412 x 915 px phone. Tinted outline around "Home ⇄ CAS" = the enlarged hit area. Nothing here is built.

Common

  • No Home key and no CAS key. The status line's place name is a button: one tap switches Home and CAS. Its tap target is 96 x 48 px (accepted by the owner, 2026-10-14): the line plus 20 px below it, which takes taps from the top-left corner of the history. R15b checks this against tapping into a formula.
  • The Mode sheet is a chip on the line (as built). Mode is also on Shift + Apps (a keys-only path, S2); Settings stays the last row of Mode.
  • The Apps sheet lists 1 Home, 2 CAS, then the apps, so Home and CAS work by keys alone too.
  • The maths rows 3 to 9 do not move. Esc, Cancel (Shift = Undo), View (Shift = Copy) and Menu (Shift = Paste) keep their ids and layers.

How D is ordered

No key you know moves: every key that stays is at its G2 place. The removed Home and CAS keys and today's gap become the three free keys, so all 12 cells are used.

ColumnKeys, top to bottomRole
1Apps, U1, U2Where to go: the app list, your own key, then Home (U2's default).
2Symb, Plot, NumThe three views of the open app, in the G2's order.
3–4▲ / ◀ ▶ / ▼The pad, as today (trackpad drag included).
5Help, View, MenuAbout what is on screen. Help is drawn dimmed and gives the dead-key haptic until context help exists (milestone 6), so the cell is never a hole.
6Esc, Cancel, U3Back out, then CAS (U3's default).

The free keys U1, U2, U3

  • Defaults (owner, 2026-10-14): U2 = Home and U3 = CAS, in their G2 cells, so a G2 hand keeps both keys; the status-line tap stays the faster way, and both keys can be set to something else. U1 ships empty.
  • Six actions: each key has its tap and its Shift layer (flick up), so U1 to U3 hold six actions.
  • What one can hold: anything Find lists (a function, which takes a selection as its argument like every key does (R17), a constant, a variable, a unit from milestone 5), or a place (an app, Mode, a Tmpl cell).
  • Empty: drawn as a dashed “＋”. A tap opens Find titled “Set U1”; the pick is stored, and from then on the key fires it.
  • Change or clear: Settings › User keys, a list of the six slots. There is no new gesture.
  • By us: defaults can ship in spec/keyboard.yaml. Tonight they ship empty, so a future feature gets a key here without moving any other key. The user's choice wins over the default.
  • Stored with the settings in IndexedDB, so the keys survive a relaunch.
  • Build order: R15b draws the keys and stores the slots. Choosing a slot's action uses Find, so that comes with R4 (Find UI). Until then an empty key gives the dead-key haptic.
  • Removal test (docs/03 rule 13): if, after a few weeks of daily use, no slot has been set, the keys go back to being gaps.

The fallback if the line is slow

If after a week the 28 px line feels far from the thumb, the switch also goes on the keypad without a new key: a flick down on Apps goes to Home or CAS, whichever is not open (the Shift layer is already Mode). The Apps sheet path (Apps, 1 or 2) works from day one.

Recommendation

D with 48 px cluster rows. It keeps the cross and every key at its G2 place, leaves no empty cell, and gives back about 3 points of viewport. The three free keys are where new things go without moving any other key. If 48 px feels small on the phone, go back to today's height; nothing else changes.
