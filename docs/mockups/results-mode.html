2 · Results mode: what the collapsed viewport shows

The keypad is collapsed to the 44 px slice (S9, docs/archive/r13-results-mode.md 1.1): the viewport is 767 px at 412 × 839 and 668 px at 360 × 740 (the entry line, 79.6 px, stays; history 687.4 px, 635.4 picked, geometry and r13 1.1). The third row is picked and shows the line of six (Insert, Recall, Copy, Store ▶, Delete, Plot; 52 px, one line above the entry line, r13 1.3). Plot is dimmed because the row is not a CAS row (R53 item 7). The five steps are an RC filter; the values and σ are made up for the picture. Row heights of a bare row (64.8 px, pitch 74.8) are measured (r19 part 1); the name tag (16 px), the reason line (16 px) and W2's one-line row (34 px) are drawn, not measured.

W (recommended): today's rows plus a name tag over the input of a stored row (v = 20 m/s over 72 km/h → m/s), σ muted beside the result, ⚠ with its reason line. W2: one line per step, name = result unit, the formula muted at the right; it loses the 2D result (a fraction or a root is not drawn stacked). A step's name here is its stored name, so what W shows beyond today is the name and, with the named-values change (r19 part 1, R53c), the unit and σ the name keeps.

3 · The viewport room

The keypad takes 514 px of 839 today: padding 8 + three cluster rows of 36 + seven maths rows of 48 + nine gaps of 6 + padding 8 (geometry), and the viewport (history + the 79.6 px entry line) is what is left of the window after the 28 px status line. The dashed amber line is the 0.35 floor (28 + 0.35 × window): the viewport's lower edge must reach it. The hatched band is the 52 px line of a picked row (geometry): in R1, R2 and today it is taken from the history; with R3 it lies over the empty entry line, so the history does not move. The keypad is drawn at its rows and gaps, unlabelled; the splits of R1 (rows 42 and 32) and R2 (gaps and padding 4) are the options' CSS in geometry --options, and the sum is its keypad height. History rows are 1÷3 (pitch 86.8 px, from 217 px = 2.50 rows).
