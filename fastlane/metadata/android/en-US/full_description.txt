Pascaline is a graphing calculator with a computer algebra system, for engineers and students. It works completely offline, has no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and is built entirely from open source.

It uses the logical key layout of a modern graphing calculator (the same function on the same key and shift layer) with its own look. Type fractions, powers and roots the way they are written in a textbook, and get exact answers or 12-digit decimals.

Free software, GPL-3.0-or-later. Maths by Giac (the engine also used by KhiCAS), GSL, GMP and MPFR.
